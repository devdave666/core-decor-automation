Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This application ("the app") is an internal automation tool used to publish content to the Core Decor Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not a consumer product and is not made available to the public for installation or use.

What this app does

The app generates and publishes video content to accounts owned and operated by Core Decor. It does not collect, process, or store personal data belonging to any third party. It does not offer login, account creation, or any interactive feature to the public.

Data the app accesses

The app accesses only the Facebook Page and Instagram professional account that Core Decor itself owns and manages, using credentials Core Decor has authorized, for the sole purpose of publishing content that Core Decor has created. No visitor, follower, or third-party user data is collected, read, or retained by this app.
